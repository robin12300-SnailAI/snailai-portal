The person behind the systems

Systems are Robin's native language. AI just gave that instinct a set of power tools.

Built systems his whole career — with unusual instinct

Two decades of running real businesses across e-commerce, trade and property in Sydney taught Robin where a business leaks time and money — and what the system behind it wants to become. Colleagues describe an unusual aptitude: show him a messy process once, and he can already see the clean architecture underneath it. It's not training; it's how he thinks.

Frontier AI, mastered and shipped

When a new model or agent framework is released, it moves from Robin's test bench into a production system within days — not quarters. He runs hybrid cloud and local AI, knows exactly what each model is good at, and applies the frontier where it pays for itself. The result is automation capability that most businesses won't see for years.

A master who trains masters

Robin is the founder and lead instructor of Snail AI Academy, where he has personally trained a cohort of practitioners — many of whom now build and deliver client systems of their own: websites, automations and AI tools delivered to real businesses.
