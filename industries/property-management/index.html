AI for Property Management

Property management is an inbox business: tenant requests, owner queries, contractor emails, compliance documents. We help teams triage, respond and close the loop — with evidence attached.

What we automate for property teams

📥

Email & document triage

Incoming emails and attachments classified by property, urgency and type — routed to the right person, first time.

🔧

Maintenance request intake

Tenant requests captured with property, issue and urgency, cross-checked against lease and contractor info, queued for action.

✉️

Owner & tenant comms drafting

Updates, approvals and follow-ups drafted in your voice for review — consistent, timely, one click to approve.

🧾

Evidence-based task completion

Every completed task carries its evidence — photos, invoices, emails — so tribunal and audit requests are answered in minutes.

📊

Reporting & document retrieval

Owner reports assembled from data you already hold; any document findable by property, date or tenant in seconds.

🔌

Property platform integration

Working with your existing property management software through official integrations — not around it.

Built for the realities of the job

  • Nothing sends to owners or tenants without team approval — communication controls are documented up front
  • Tenant and owner personal information is handled strictly within your property platform and approved systems
  • Urgent-safety and urgent-repair flows always route to a human immediately — automation never delays an urgent repair
  • Works with the portfolio size you have today; scales without re-platforming later
