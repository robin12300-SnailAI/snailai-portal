The boundaries we work within

Medical work has hard limits. These aren't disclaimers — they're how we scope every clinic engagement.

  • The public website never connects directly to clinical databases or patient record systems
  • We don't process unauthorised patient clinical information — data access is scoped, documented and approved before any workflow touches it
  • AI does not make medical diagnoses or treatment decisions, ever
  • We prefer vendor-supplied widgets, hosted pages and official APIs over any custom workaround
  • The boundary between clinical systems and administrative systems is drawn explicitly, in writing, in the proposal
  • Any patient-facing clinical content is reviewed by qualified clinical staff before publication
