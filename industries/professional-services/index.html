Professional standards, engineered in

Firms in accounting, law and finance have obligations that don't bend for convenience. Our workflows respect them by design.

  • All client-facing drafts are human-reviewed before sending — no exceptions
  • Client confidential information stays within your approved systems; we document where data lives before any workflow is built
  • Advice, tax positions and legal positions remain the professional's responsibility — AI drafts, humans decide
  • Regulated firms receive a data-risk review during discovery covering privacy, confidentiality and record-keeping obligations
