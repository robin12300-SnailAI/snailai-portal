Service

AI Workflow Automation

Automate the repetitive work that slows your team down — while keeping a human in control of anything that touches your customers or your compliance obligations.

Typical scenarios we automate

Every automation is designed around a workflow you can name, measured by hours returned and errors avoided.

📧

Email triage & drafting

Incoming emails classified, summarised and drafted for review — nothing sends without approval.

📄

Document intake & classification

Invoices, forms and attachments captured, named and routed to the right place automatically.

🔁

Customer follow-up

Structured follow-up sequences for quotes, bookings and service requests, drafted and queued for sign-off.

🔎

Internal knowledge retrieval

Ask questions of your own documents and SOPs instead of digging through folders.

📊

Reporting & recurring summaries

Weekly and monthly reports assembled from data you already have, on a schedule.

✅

Human-approved communications

High-risk messages require explicit approval before anything leaves the building.

🗂️

Task & evidence tracking

Work items tracked with evidence attached — who did what, when, with what document.

What's included

  • Workflow discovery and documentation of the current process
  • Automation design with clear human approval points
  • Implementation against your existing systems
  • Testing with real (non-sensitive) sample data
  • Team training and written SOP
  • Ongoing monitoring and optimisation options

What this is not

  • "Fully unmanned" operation — we don't promise zero humans, and you wouldn't want that for customer-facing work
  • Automatic payments — payments stay with your approved, hosted payment provider and a human trigger
  • Unauthorised outbound messaging — nothing sends externally without documented approval rules
  • Processing of data you don't have permission to use — we confirm permissions, storage location and retention rules before touching customer data
