Service

Business AI Assessment

A structured, low-commitment path from "we think AI could help" to a prioritised proposal with scope, phases and pricing — so you never buy a solution looking for a problem.

How the assessment works

Four stages, each with a clear purpose. You can stop after any of them.

1

Business AI Scan

A structured 10-minute intake that captures your goals, current systems, repetitive workflows and constraints. It builds the one-page opportunity summary we work from.

Start the Scan →
2

Confirmation call

A focused 5–10 minute call to verify the key facts — the outcome you want, who'd be involved, which system hurts most, and your timing.

3

On-site assessment

Qualified Sydney businesses receive a complimentary two-hour on-site assessment led by our founder: real workflows, real people, real systems.

4

Proposal

Priorities, scope, phases, pricing, exclusions and success criteria — written down, with a clear next step.

What the on-site assessment covers

  • Business goals, project goals and what "success" means to you (15 min)
  • Current processes, main difficulties and repetitive work (30 min)
  • Your website, CRM, booking, email and document systems (30 min)
  • Opportunity identification, risks, priorities and feasible paths (30 min)
  • Budget range, project phases and how a proposal would be structured (15 min)
What the assessment is not: it is not free detailed system design, and not every applicant receives an on-site visit. Complex medical, legal or financial engagements may include an additional data-risk review, and any recording or transcription only happens with explicit consent from everyone in the room.
