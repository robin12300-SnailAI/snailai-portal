Clear system boundaries — by design

We keep four layers separate and explicit in every proposal, so nothing sensitive ends up where it shouldn't.

Layer 1

Public website (presentation)

Content, services, SEO. No customer records, no clinical data, no credentials.

Layer 2

Third-party booking & payment

Vendors' official widgets, hosted pages and APIs. Card data never touches your site.

Layer 3

Internal customer / clinical databases

Stay internal. A public website never connects directly to these systems.

Layer 4

AI-assisted work systems

Automation that helps staff draft, summarise and retrieve — with human approval gates.
