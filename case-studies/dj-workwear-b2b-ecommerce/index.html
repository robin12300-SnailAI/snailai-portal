DJ Workwear: B2B E-commerce and Quote Workflow

A live Australian workwear website designed around how business customers browse products, request branded-workwear quotes and complete online purchases.

IndustryB2B workwear and uniforms
ScopeWebsite · Product catalogue · Quote capture · Checkout · Admin · SEO
LocationSydney, serving Australia-wide
StatusLive

The business problem

The business needed one professional web presence for product discovery, quote enquiries and online purchasing. Product information had to be easy for customers to browse and practical for the business to maintain.

What Snail AI delivered

  • A mobile-first catalogue organised by product category
  • Individual product pages with stock and price information
  • A structured quote path for bulk and branded orders
  • Cart and hosted Stripe checkout for online payment
  • Admin tools for products and site content
  • Technical SEO foundations and practical buying guides

System boundary

Card details remain with Stripe's hosted payment flow. The public site contains product and business content, while administrative access is restricted to approved users.

What can be verified publicly

The production website is live, customers can browse the catalogue, request a quote and complete an online checkout, and the business can publish product and guide content. Commercial performance metrics are not published without an agreed measurement period and client approval.
