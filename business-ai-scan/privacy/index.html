Privacy Notice

How we handle your information when you use the AI Business Opportunity Scan.

Effective date: August 2026

1. What We Collect

We collect the following information through the assessment form:

  • Business name and suburb
  • Industry sector
  • Contact details (name, email, phone)
  • Current tools and software used
  • Workflow descriptions
  • AI readiness preferences

We do NOT collect or accept: personal health information, financial records, identity documents, or client/patient data. Please do not enter this type of information in the assessment.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to generate your AI Opportunity Report. Submitted information is processed by a third-party AI model (language model API) to produce the analysis.

We do not use your submission for any other purpose without your consent.

3. How Your Report Is Generated

Your report is generated by Snail AI's own scoring system, hosted on the same infrastructure described in our main Privacy Policy. Your responses are not sent to any third-party AI or language model provider, and are not used to train any model.

The assessment asks only for business contact and operational details — your name, work email, mobile number, company name and website, location, role, headcount, industry and systems used. We do not ask for Medicare numbers, tax file numbers, driver licence or passport details, bank or card details, or health records. Please do not enter personal information about your clients, customers, tenants, employees or patients.

4. Data Security

  • Secure report links use non-guessable tokens
  • Reports are not indexed by search engines
  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS
  • Access to submitted data is restricted to authorised Snail AI personnel

5. Data Retention

Unconverted submissions are automatically deleted after 90 days unless you request longer retention or we have a legitimate reason to retain them.

You can request earlier deletion at any time.

6. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your submission data at any time by contacting us.

Report links expire after 30 days by default but can be revoked earlier upon request.

7. Marketing Communications

Marketing emails require separate, optional consent. Receiving your report does not subscribe you to marketing.

You can unsubscribe from marketing at any time.

8. Medical and Healthcare Submissions

For healthcare practices, we only assess non-clinical administrative workflows. We do not collect, process or store patient health information.

If you accidentally submit health information, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.
