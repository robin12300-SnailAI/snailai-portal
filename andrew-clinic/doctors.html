Dr. Andrew Li

MBBS, FRACGP, Dip SCCA, Cert AAAM

In 2001 he joined the training program for the Royal Australian College of General Practitioners. During this time he spent 2 years in North Queensland where he treated thousands of patients with skin cancer and gained extensive experience in this area. He successfully completed all requirements of the college and was awarded a Fellowship of the Royal Australian College of General Practitioners in 2004. Dr Li has also pursued further studies specific to Skin Cancer Medicine. He successfully completed all requirements of the Skin Cancer College Australasia and was awarded a Diploma of Skin Cancer Medicine in 2010. In order to achieve highest possible accuracy of diagnosing early skin cancer, Dr Li has also completed an advanced skin cancer diagnosed course through the Skin Cancer College Australasia and has been awarded a Certificate of Dermoscopy. Over the years he has successfully treated tens of thousands of patients with all forms of skin cancers range from early to advanced skin cancers, from melanoma to non melanoma skin cancers such as basal cell carcinoma and squamous cell carcinoma using surgical and non surgical treatment techniques. His high level of surgical skills and expertise in the areas of skin cancer medicine and male circumcision enable his patients to achieve best possible treatment outcomes in almost all cases that he has treated.

Mr. Omid Zarbaft

Dermal Clinician

As a dedicated dermal clinician, I specialise in advanced skin treatments and aesthetic enhancements, utilising high-end pico laser devices to deliver precise and effective results. With a strong foundation in medical and biological sciences, I have cultivated a particular passion for the skin—the body's largest and most complex organ. Over the past five years at Drummoyne Cosmetic and Skin Cancer Clinic in Sydney, I have honed my expertise through hands-on experience, complemented by recurrent professional training at the Australian Academy of Cosmetic Dermal Science. My practice reflects a commitment to combining scientific knowledge with cutting-edge technology to achieve optimal skin health and aesthetic outcomes.
